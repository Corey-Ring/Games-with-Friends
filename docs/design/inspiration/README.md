# Design Inspiration — Retro Maximalist

Source photographs behind [`GamesWithFriends/ART_DIRECTION.md`](../../../GamesWithFriends/ART_DIRECTION.md).
Drop the photo files into this folder using the names below (they were shared in chat
on 2026-08-22; export from Photos and rename to match).

Each entry says what to **take** from the photo and what to **ignore** — the photos are
inspiration for an original style, not assets to trace or brands to imitate.

## Photos

### `maeve-bonbon-box.jpg` — Seattle Chocolate "Maeve" bonbon mix box (yellow)
- **Take:** the whole thesis. Mustard ground; chunky white logo with notched letterforms
  in a framed band; dense factory-scene illustration with uniform ink outlines and flat
  candy fills; a mascot (hard-hat bear) waving over the product-name lozenge; the
  cream lozenge holding "Chocolate Bonbon Mix"; window cut showing the goods.
- **Ignore:** price sticker, brand names, the specific letterform notching (ours is
  Shrikhand, not a Maeve-alike).

### `maeve-pink-bubbly.jpg` — Maeve "Pink Bubbly" box top (yellow/pink/green)
- **Take:** the small-scale motif system — quarter-circle sunbursts, pink daisies with
  green centers, white dots, triangle runs in the margins; the three-layer lockup
  (small label panel → "presents" pill → big framed logo → product lozenge); pink+green
  as a frame duo on yellow.
- **Ignore:** brand text, the die-cut box shape.

### `fishwife-tins.jpg` — Fishwife tinned-seafood boxes on a shelf
- **Take:** how one design system flexes per flavor: same anatomy, swapped ground color
  per SKU (pink / magenta / plum) — this is the model for per-game accent identity.
  Ribbon banners carrying the product name; the crest/portrait medallion; hand-lettered
  script logo; tiny narrative icons (boats, hooks, flowers) filling every margin;
  yellow edge-frames tying the family together.
- **Ignore:** the script logotype style (too close to their brand), fish subject matter.

### `window-flowers-soccer.jpg` — Hand-painted shop window, flowers with soccer-ball centers
- **Take:** the hand-painted energy — imperfect fat petals, flat poster colors, smiley
  faces inside flowers, scattered confetti of dots/hearts/sparkles on open ground;
  playful object substitution (ball as flower center) — license for game-themed motif
  swaps (dice-center daisies, etc.).
- **Ignore:** the sports theme, the artist signature.

### `mural-coming-soon.jpg` — "Quality American Made Goods / Coming Soon!" window mural
- **Take:** 70s bubble-letter headline treatment (yellow with dark keyline); a full
  scene as background texture (sun, rainbow, pines, town) that stays legible behind
  text; grinning anthropomorphized everything (car, sun, moon, butterflies); how dense
  a composition can get while one message still dominates.
- **Ignore:** the specific slogan/wordmark, americana framing.

### `mural-town-closeup.jpg` — Mural close-up, characters waving from buildings
- **Take:** character drawing style — big heads, dot eyes, huge friendly waves; flat
  building facades in alternating candy colors with patterned window frames; flowers
  and butterflies as sky-filler; the tight 6-color repeating palette across a busy scene.
- **Ignore:** literal cityscape subject; period hairstyles/fashion details.

## Working rules

- These photos calibrate *vibe and construction*, not exact assets. New artwork is
  always original: our palette (`ART_DIRECTION.md` §3.1), our motifs (§7), our faces.
- Do not reproduce logos, wordmarks, mascots, or trade dress from any photo.
- When adding a new reference photo: add the file here plus a take/ignore entry.
